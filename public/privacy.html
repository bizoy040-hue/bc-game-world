Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

BC GAME WORLD is an independent website that collects applications from people interested in the BC Game Agent Program. It is not operated by BC.Game. Commission figures shown on this site are program claims and are subject to official BC.Game terms and eligibility.

What we collect

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Country
  • Phone number
  • Telegram username
  • WhatsApp number
  • The date and time of your submission

How we use it

We use this information only to review your agent application and to contact you about it. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who can see it

Submitted information is stored in a private database and is visible only to authorised administrators who sign in to the dashboard. It is never shown publicly.

How we protect it

The site uses HTTPS in production, password-protected admin access, hashed admin passwords, server-side validation and rate limiting.

Retention and deletion

We keep applications only as long as needed to process them. To have your information deleted or corrected, contact us using the details you received from our team, and we will act on your request.

Consent

By ticking the consent box on the registration form, you agree to this processing. You can withdraw consent at any time by asking us to delete your application.
